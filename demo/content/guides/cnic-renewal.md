NADRA handles CNIC renewals. Start at [nadra.gov.pk](https://www.nadra.gov.pk) and confirm the current fee and process.

## Documents required

- Old CNIC, if available
- Mobile number linked to you

## Step-by-step procedure

1. Open the NADRA portal or visit a NADRA center
2. Select CNIC renewal
3. Verify your details
4. Pay the fee
5. Track the status and collect your card
