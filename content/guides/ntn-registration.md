An NTN is registered online through the FBR IRIS portal at [iris.fbr.gov.pk](https://iris.fbr.gov.pk). Confirm current requirements on the official site before you start.

## Documents required

- CNIC
- Active mobile number and email address
- Proof of business (for a business NTN)

## Step-by-step procedure

1. Open the FBR IRIS portal
2. Create an account with your CNIC, mobile number and email
3. Fill in the registration form
4. Upload the required documents
5. Receive your NTN certificate
